FAQ

Questions, answered directly.

Is BrightBox dropshipping?

BrightBox uses supplier-connected ecommerce infrastructure, but the service is positioned as an advanced reselling system rather than a generic dropshipping course or store package.

Do I need to hold inventory?

The BrightBox model is designed to reduce the need for the reseller to purchase and store inventory upfront. Supplier arrangements and product availability vary.

Does BrightBox fulfill my orders?

No. BrightBox builds the ecommerce infrastructure and automation around the operation. Physical fulfillment is handled by the selected supplier or fulfillment partner.

Do I need Shopify?

BrightBox is primarily designed around Shopify storefront infrastructure.

Can I sell on marketplaces too?

The system can complement other channels, but BrightBox’s core strategy is to help you establish your own ecommerce storefront rather than depend entirely on third-party marketplaces.

Are profits guaranteed?

No. BrightBox does not guarantee sales or profits. Results depend on product selection, pricing, marketing, execution, market conditions and other factors.

Do I need previous reselling experience?

BrightBox is primarily designed for people who already have some experience with reselling and want to upgrade their infrastructure.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your operation and requirements. Apply and the team will walk you through the options.
